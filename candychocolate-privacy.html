1. Introduction

Welcome to Candy Chocolate Swapper ("the App", "we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our mobile game.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Collected Automatically

When you play Candy Chocolate Swapper, certain information is collected automatically by our advertising partners and analytics services:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, language settings, and time zone.
  • Advertising ID: Your device's Advertising ID (Google Play Services ID / Advertising Identifier) is collected by ad networks to serve personalized ads.
  • IP Address: Your general location is derived from your IP address for ad targeting and fraud prevention.
  • Gameplay Data: Your score, level progress, coins earned, rounds played, and game session duration are stored locally on your device only.

2.2 Information You Provide Voluntarily

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to play Candy Chocolate Swapper.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Display advertisements within the App through third-party ad networks.
  • Analyze gameplay trends to improve game performance and user experience.
  • Save your game progress (score, coins, levels) locally on your device so you can continue where you left off.
  • Prevent fraud and invalid ad traffic.
