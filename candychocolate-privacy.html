SKM STUDIO • PRIVACY POLICY

Candy Chocolate Swapper

Developer: SKM Studio  |  Package: com.skmstudio.candysweep  |  Effective Date: November 25, 2026

SKM Studio built Candy Chocolate Swapper as a mobile casual puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Offline Operation & Local Storage

Version 1.0 of Candy Chocolate Swapper operates 100% offline. Your game progress, personal best score, and audio/vibration preferences are stored strictly locally on your device using on-device storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any SKM Studio server.

2. Personal Data Collection

SKM Studio does not directly collect, sell, rent, or store personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos).

3. Google Play Services & Google Play Billing

The app is distributed through Google Play and integrates standard Google Play Services and Google Play Billing infrastructure to support optional in-app coin pack purchases when enabled in future updates. Any payment transactions are processed securely by Google Play under Google's own Privacy Policy. SKM Studio never receives or stores your credit card, UPI, or banking details.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Advertising (v1.0)

Version 1.0 contains no third-party advertising SDKs and displays no third-party ads. If advertising features are introduced in a future update, this Privacy Policy and our Google Play Data Safety disclosures will be updated accordingly before release.

5. Children's Privacy

Candy Chocolate Swapper is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under the age of 13 without parental consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

6. Data Deletion

Because game scores and preferences are stored only locally on your device, you can clear all stored game data anytime by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
